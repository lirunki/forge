Forge — Privacy Policy

App: Forge · Package: com.forge.live
Platform: Android · Distribution: Google Play (paid) and F-Droid / direct download (free)
Effective date: August 17, 2026
This policy applies to the Forge app (the "App") published on Google Play and distributed via F-Droid and direct download. The App is a local runtime that lets you create and run small "mini-apps" (HTML) on your phone, optionally using AI services you configure yourself with your own API keys.

1. Summary

Forge is designed to keep your data on your device. The App does not require an account, does not collect your email, does not use advertising, and does not sell your data. The only data that leaves your device is sent to AI providers that you choose and authenticate with your own API key (for example, when you ask Forge to generate or run a mini-app that uses AI). Camera, microphone, and location data are only accessed when you, through a mini-app, actively use those features.

3. Data the App does NOT collect

  • Account information / email: Forge has no account system and does not read your Google account.
  • SMS, call logs, contacts: The Google Play build of Forge does not request or access SMS, call, or contacts permissions. (The optional direct-download/F-Droid build may request these for power-user mini-apps; that build is distributed outside Google Play and is governed by this same policy where applicable.)
  • Device identifiers / advertising IDs: Forge does not collect them.
  • Financial information: Forge does not process payments; purchases are handled by Google Play.
  • Web browsing history: Not collected.
  • Health, calendar, fitness data: Not collected.

4. Third parties you may connect

Forge is BYO-AI: you provide your own API key for a provider of your choice. When you use an AI feature, Forge sends the relevant text, images, or audio over HTTPS to that provider. Forge does not operate these providers and has no access to the data once it leaves your device. Each provider's privacy policy governs your data there.

Providers you can configure include, for example:

  • Google Gemini (Google AI Studio)
  • OpenAI
  • Groq
  • xAI (Grok)
  • OpenRouter
  • Any OpenAI-compatible endpoint you configure (including "Cheaper Inference")

Your API keys are stored only on your device (and in your optional backup, see §5). They are never sent to Forge's developer or any party other than the provider they belong to.

5. Optional Google Drive / folder backup

Forge can back up your mini-app library and AI settings to a folder you choose using Android's Storage Access Framework (e.g. a folder in your own Google Drive). Forge only reads/writes inside the folder you pick; it never has access to the rest of your Drive. The backup contains your API keys — do not share the backup folder with anyone. Disconnecting backup removes Forge's access to that folder.

6. Data transmission & security

  • Network traffic to AI providers uses HTTPS/TLS.
  • API keys are stored in the app's private storage, not in plain shared files.
  • Backup data is written only to the folder you explicitly select.

7. Data retention & deletion

  • On your device: mini-apps, settings, and keys persist until you delete them in the App or clear the App's data from Android Settings. Deleting a mini-app removes it locally.
  • Backup folder: you can delete the backed-up files yourself at any time, or disconnect the folder in Forge settings.
  • At AI providers: retention is governed by each provider's policy and your account there. Forge cannot delete data held by third-party providers; manage it in your provider account.

8. Children

Forge is a general-purpose developer tool and is not directed at children under 13 (or the applicable age in your country). Forge does not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has provided data, contact us so it can be removed.

9. Your rights

Because Forge keeps your data on your device and with providers you control, you exercise your rights directly: review or delete mini-apps in the App, clear app data in Android Settings, disconnect or delete your backup folder, and manage your data in your AI provider accounts.

10. Permissions used by the Google Play build

For transparency, the Google Play build requests:

Internet, Camera, Record audio, Access fine location, Access coarse location, Post notifications, Bluetooth connect, Foreground service (data sync), Wake lock, Vibrate, Receive boot completed, Read media images, Modify audio settings, Access network state, Access Wi-Fi state, Change Wi-Fi state, Nearby Wi-Fi devices, Schedule / use exact alarm.

The Google Play build does not request SMS, call-log, contacts, phone-state, or query-all-packages permissions.
